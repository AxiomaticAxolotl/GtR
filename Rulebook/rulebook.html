Buildings

Start. With a Craftsman or Architect action, play a building from your hand as a foundation and take a site of its material to go with it. You can’t start a building if you already have one with the same name.

Out of town. Once a material’s in-town sites are gone, a foundation of that material goes on an out-of-town site and costs two actions instead of one.

Build. Each further action adds one material that matches the site, tucked under the foundation: from your hand (Craftsman) or your stockpile (Architect). A building needs as many materials as its value — 1, 2 or 3. The foundation card doesn’t count.

Complete. The moment the last material goes in, the building is finished. Its power applies from now on, and its site moves to your influence, raising your clientele and vault limits.

Value. Rubble and Wood are worth 1, Concrete and Brick 2, Marble and Stone 3. It is the number of materials a building needs and the influence its site gives.

Card text beats these rules. A building’s power only works once it is complete.

5

Game End

The game ends immediately when:

  • the last card is drawn from the deck;
  • the in-town sites of all six materials have run out;
  • a Catacomb is completed; or
  • a completed Forum or Forum Romanum meets its condition — its owner wins outright.

Scoring

  • Influence: 1 VP for each point of influence.
  • Vault: each material is worth its value — 1, 2 or 3 VP.
  • Merchant bonus: 3 VP per material to whoever has the most of it in their vault. Tied? Nobody gets it.
  • Buildings: any VP granted by your completed buildings.

Most VP wins. Ties go to the tied player with the most cards in hand.

Easy to forget. Every action is optional. There is no maximum hand size — 5 is only how far you refill. Clients and vault cards are capped by influence; the stockpile isn’t. Nobody may look at cards in a vault, not even its owner.

POCKET RULES
2–5 players  ·  about an hour

Citizens of Rome! Caesar demands ACTION!

64 AD: Rome is in turmoil – the city has been devastated by fire. Emperor Nero is racing back from Antium to start rebuilding Structures lost to the flames. Caesar expects your aid in his historic effort to restore Glory To Rome.

Setup

  1. Give each player a camp. Set out the Jacks and merchant bonuses.
  2. Sort the sites by material. For each material, stack one in-town site per player on top of the rest, flipped to their out-of-town side.
  3. Play the Republic or the Imperium version, never both: box up the other version’s marked cards. Shuffle the order cards and deal each player 5 (Imperium: 4 and a Jack).
  4. Deal one card per player face up into the pool in the middle of the table. Whoever’s card comes first alphabetically takes the Leader card.

Your Camp

Influence (top): sites of your completed buildings. You start with 2; each site adds its value.
Clientele (left): hired clients. Limit = influence.
Vault (right): face-down loot, scored at game end. Limit = influence.
Stockpile (bottom): building materials. No limit.
2

The Turn

The Leader either leads a role or thinks.

Lead. Play a card from your hand and announce its role. Going clockwise, every other player either follows by playing a card of that same role, or thinks.

Think. Choose one:

  • draw from the deck until you hold 5 cards;
  • draw 1 card, if you already hold 5 or more; or
  • take a Jack.

If the Leader thinks, no role is led and the turn ends there.

Act. Starting with the Leader and going clockwise, each player performs the led role’s action: once for a card they led or followed with, plus once for each client of that role in their clientele. Clients act even if you thought. Finish all your actions before the next player starts.

Clean up. Order cards played to lead or follow go to the pool; Jacks go back to their pile. Pass the Leader card to the left.

Wild cards. A Jack can be played as any role. So can a petition: two order cards that share a role, played together (Imperium: three).

The Six Roles

Laborer  Rubble · yellow 1
Take 1 card from the pool into your stockpile.
Craftsman  Wood · green 1
Start a building, or build with 1 card from your hand.
Architect  Concrete · grey 2
Start a building, or build with 1 card from your stockpile.
Legionary  Brick · red 2
Show (and keep) a card from hand to demand its material: take 1 matching card from the pool and from each neighbor’s hand into your stockpile. A neighbor with none says “Glory to Rome!”
Patron  Marble · purple 3
Take 1 card from the pool into your clientele.
Merchant  Stone · blue 3
Move 1 material from your stockpile to your vault, face down.

The number in the black dot is the material’s value.
